<h1>Chimney</h1>
<h2>These are my collection of single file .html games, along with Gust. Below is a complete guide on how to host them on multiple websites that aren't normally covered by other guides.</h2>
<h3>Local (file://)</h3>
This is the simplest way to play these games, although your IT department at your district probably knows about it already.
<h4>1.)</h4>
Click the "Code" button on https://github.com/kwuirry/chimneyassets<br>
<img width="107" height="30" alt="Screenshot_20261025_125931" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/141dcd13-c4bb-49e0-9ca6-766de28b89bb" /><br>
<h4>2.)</h4>
Click "Download ZIP" at the bottom of the dropdown menu.<br>
<img width="400" height="331" alt="Screenshot_20261025_130146" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/fee2746f-38e7-4ca3-9205-299205a6aeda" /><br>
<h4>3.)</h4>
Right click the downloaded zip in the downloads folder (or wherever you saved the ZIP to) and click Extract Here/Uncompress. (Depends on your operating system)
<h5>4.)</h5>
Double-click the folder that was created and double click any .html file you wish to open in your browser. Enjoy!<br><br>
